# ritesh

register: brand

## Product purpose

ritesh is rite, a shell for the browser on xterm.js. Sites embed it so visitors can type into a page: move around a filesystem the site defines, run its commands, and find a few secrets. It started as a 2012 homepage experiment and is now an open-source library (npm `riteshjs`).

## Users

Developers who embed it in their own sites (docs, portfolios, product pages), and the visitors of those sites, who meet it as a terminal and type `help`.

## Brand

Playful, mechanical, a little ceremonial: a rite, not a tool. Personality comes from what the shell answers (easter eggs, honest errors), not from decoration.

## Anti-references

Green-on-black hacker terminals, neon cyberpunk, SaaS landing pages with feature cards, editorial-magazine type.

## Principles

- The terminal is the product; the page frames it and gets out of the way.
- Every word earns its place. No em dashes.
- Works with a keyboard alone; Esc then Tab leaves the terminal.
